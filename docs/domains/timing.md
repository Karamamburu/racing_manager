# Domain: timing

## Responsibility

Live manual timing of protocol categories: attempt lifecycle, number and tile capture, pending finishes, official-time corrections, the event-wide capture lock, closure with result and lap-split publication, and event-scoped access through the global `REFEREE` role.

## Public contracts

- **Roles:** `REFEREE` is a global product role; `ADMINISTRATOR` has the complete timing capability set; `ORGANIZER` alone has no timing surface. A user holds a set of roles and capabilities are the union. `PATCH /admin/users/:userId/referee-role` (`SetRefereeRoleDto.enabled`) grants or removes `REFEREE` for an administrator.
- **Attempt identity:** `event × start × format × gender × attemptNumber`. At most one `RUNNING` attempt per category; a cancelled attempt frees the category for a new attempt, a completed or incomplete one does not.
- **Surface:** `GET /events/:eventId/timing` → `TimingSurfaceView` with one `TimingCategoryBlockView` per timeable category (ordered by start, format, gender) and the capture-lock holder. `GET /events/:eventId/timing/attempts/:attemptId` → `TimingAttemptRecordView`. The page header keeps a «К мероприятию» action back to the event page.
- **Block cues:** a running block reports `capturesComplete` when every non-excluded participant holds exactly N bound passes and no finish is unbound; the page then shows the blue «Все отсечки … получены, её можно завершить» prompt in place of the running-attempt warning. A finished protocol category (or a finished multi-stage race) reports `finished`; its locked-results reason is shown as a green plate. `capturesComplete` is false outside a running attempt.
- **Judge summary:** the top block shows a live summary computed from the surface read with no extra request: categories finished/total (by `finished`), participants finished/total (unique timeable enrollments holding N passes), participants on course (`RACING`), blocks ready to complete (`capturesComplete`), outstanding unassigned splits plus unbound finishes, and passes still to capture in running blocks. Participants outside timing and race-section duplicates are not counted.
- **Start:** `POST /events/:eventId/timing/attempts` (`StartTimingAttemptDto`) freezes the roster (enrollment, event-unique number, section, existing outcome) and records `officialStartedAt`; the response carries the first `ClockEpochView`. N is the start `lapCount`. A start is refused for no timeable participant, a missing number, a finished category, a completed/incomplete previous attempt, a cancelled/past-completed event or a workstation without the lock.
- **Capture:** `POST /events/:eventId/timing/passes` (`CaptureTimingPassDto`, `Idempotency-Key`) with kind `NUMBER`, `TILE` or `PENDING_FINISH`; a number auto-routes to the single running enrollment with fewer than N passes, asks for a choice only when two or more qualify, and otherwise creates nothing with a reason. Every capture mutation carries a per-workstation `deviceSequence`; `timing_capture_receipts` records applied/rejected sequences and `CompleteTimingAttemptDto.lastIssuedSequence` proves the outgoing queue is resolved.
- **Pending finishes:** `POST .../pending-finishes/:id/bind`, `POST .../pending-finishes/:id/cancel` (administrator + reason), and `POST .../attempts/:attemptId/undo` (quick undo of the last pass creation of that workstation only).
- **Clock:** a `ClockEpoch` anchors the client monotonic clock to an official elapsed value; reload/transfer opens a new epoch and continues the real elapsed time. `POST .../attempts/:attemptId/clock-epochs` returns `ClockEpochView`.
- **Corrections:** `PATCH .../passes/:passId/official-time`, `POST .../passes/:passId/cancel`, `POST .../passes/:passId/rebind` are administrator-only, require a reason and never rewrite the raw capture. A pass instant must be strictly after the official start and a total at most 24 hours.
- **Closure:** `POST .../attempts/:attemptId/complete`, `.../force-complete` (administrator + reason → `INCOMPLETE`), `.../cancel` (administrator + reason → `CANCELLED`, publishes nothing). Closure is atomic: attempt state, every eligible enrollment result, N splits and the closure report commit together or not at all.
- **Publication:** a closed `COMPLETED` attempt writes, for every non-excluded active participant, an authoritative enrollment result: `FINISHED` at N with the total and exactly N `TimingLapSplitDto` entries, `DNS` when no capture exists, or `DNF` when at least one capture exists but fewer than N. An `INCOMPLETE` forced closure publishes only the participants already at N, as before; a cancelled attempt publishes nothing. `Result.timing_session_id` records provenance. Timing writes no place and no registration-only result.
- **Boundaries:** rows displayed in a multi-stage race section are excluded from timing and keep their heat-time flow; withdrawn enrollments and pre-existing outcomes are excluded. A start number cannot change while its registration is in a running attempt. Race creation is refused while the proposed field would capture a running-attempt participant. Legacy events without starts offer no timing. «Завершить гонку» is refused while an attempt runs and is read-only for timing after the finish.
- **Capture lock:** one event-wide row (`timing_capture_locks`) claimed explicitly by `POST .../capture/take`, moved by `.../capture/transfer` after the outgoing workstation’s receipts are confirmed, and released by an administrator through `.../capture/force-release`. A closure—ordinary, forced or cancelled—keeps the hold: the workstation that timed one category keeps capture for the next one, so a capture right after a category completion is never refused with `CAPTURE_NOT_HELD`. Releasing stays explicit. There is no automatic lease; a lost device waits for force-release. Removing `REFEREE` ends the hold on the next claim. The UI calls a workstation «устройство»: the holder's top block shows the device id with a «Копировать» control, and the transfer field is labelled «Устройство назначения» and takes that id; a transfer to the current device is refused locally.

## MANUAL-TIMING-2 delta

- **Anonymous split:** Enter or Space on the empty number field in normal capture mode creates an event-scoped anonymous split with no participant, no lap and no current block. The top control block is three full-height columns: capture controls on the left, the large square number field in the middle, and the event-wide queue on the right (stretches with the block, internal scroll, no layout shift). At capture the attempts running strictly before the press are frozen as the split's candidates. Assignment is by number only: the event-unique number resolves to a registration, then to exactly one qualifying candidate attempt; several ask for an explicit choice, none names every reason. The pass receives the captured instant expressed in the chosen attempt's scale. A `REFEREE` may cancel any unassigned split of an event they can time, an `ADMINISTRATOR` any, with a reason; quick undo retires the workstation's newest capture in the event. An unassigned split blocks completion, force-completion and cancellation of every still-running candidate. A split queued before MANUAL-TIMING-3 stays bound to its captured attempt and blocks only that attempt.
- **Heat attempt:** a multi-stage race is timed one heat at a time. The attempt is identified by event + race + `stageId` + `heatNumber` + attempt number; its roster comes from the heat's start list resolved to race-start enrollments; N is that race start's `lapCount`. A category attempt and a heat attempt of the same race can run concurrently. A heat closure writes `ClassHeatTime` total + `OK` and, when the start has event laps, one `ClassHeatLapTime` per lap from the attempt's official-instant splits; it writes no ordinary `Result`, no `result_splits`, no place, no CMS call and no stage/qualification change. The manual heat-time form and stage commit keep `ORGANIZER`/`ADMINISTRATOR` access; a running heat blocks the manual form for that heat, and stage commitment/structural stage changes are blocked while any heat of the stage runs. The category attempt still excludes race-section rows with `RACE_SECTION`.
- **Closure gate:** gates read only the closing attempt's marks, receipts, splits and participants. A capture refused by the capture-lock or clock-epoch check, and a capture, tile, split assignment, pending bind or quick undo definitively refused after those checks, writes a `REJECTED` receipt so it cannot block closure; a foreign/stale `lastIssuedSequence` is not expanded into missing sequences. A capture-less attempt closes, and an all-excluded attempt closes publishing nothing. An unassigned split, an unbound pending finish and an unconfirmed sequence refuse closure with a Russian message naming the blocker.
- **Live elapsed:** a running attempt's displayed elapsed value updates at least every 100 ms from the existing epoch anchor, with no network request and no official write; a hidden tab may update less often.
- **Timing-page blocks:** every category and heat block collapses independently, keeping its header, state tag and actions visible while its body folds away. Participant tiles keep a fixed 4:3 shape, show only the number, name and lap count, and are filled with their state colour, so more tiles fit on screen and the whole card is an easy click target. A category or heat card is tinted by gender for quick identification: light blue for men, light pink for women.
- **Block order:** the page presents category groups as `RUNNING` first, then `NOT_STARTED`, then every closed state (`COMPLETED`, `INCOMPLETE`, `CANCELLED`) last. A category block and its heat blocks move together, the backend order is kept inside one state, and a group's priority is its most active block, so a running heat lifts its multi-stage race to the top and the stage header stays next to its heats.
- **Block width:** a card sharing the row is sized by its visible participant tiles: up to 6 participants occupy a third of the row (three cards), 7–12 occupy a half (two cards), and 13 or more take the full width. Same-width cards are packed into rows, and a later card of the same state fills an earlier partial row, so a full-width race between two halves does not orphan one of them; two cards split the row exactly and a lone half or third keeps its fraction instead of stretching. A multi-stage race is one full-width section, but each stage packs its heat cards by their own roster size, so two 7–8-participant heats sit side by side. Race-section rows collapse into a note and do not widen the card. Participant tiles size themselves from the card width (128 px minimum), so a third-width card keeps readable tiles instead of shrinking to a viewport breakpoint; below 1440 px a third becomes a half and below 992 px every card is full width.
- **Per-block collapse:** each displayed protocol block has its own collapse state keyed by gender + category + section/race, expanded by default, page-session only. A block hidden by a filter loses its state and returns expanded; a rendered sibling keeps its state.
- **Routes:** `POST /events/:eventId/timing/heat-attempts` (`StartHeatAttemptDto`), `POST .../timing/splits/:splitId/assign` (`AssignAnonymousSplitDto.chosenAttemptId` for an ambiguous number), `POST .../timing/splits/:splitId/cancel`, `POST .../timing/splits/:splitId/undo` (`QuickUndoTimingSplitDto`), and `POST .../timing/passes` with `kind: 'ANONYMOUS_SPLIT'` and no `attemptId`. The existing closure/correction routes accept a heat attempt id; the category and heat closure reports add `publishedRegistrationIds`/`conflictRegistrationIds`.

## MANUAL-TIMING-3 delta

- **Block-free capture:** an `ANONYMOUS_SPLIT` capture has no `attemptId`; it is stored in `timing_event_splits` with its anchor clock epoch and the attempts running at the derived press instant frozen in `timing_anonymous_split_candidates`. A mark exists only after assignment. The open epoch of any running attempt is used as the wall-clock anchor, so the instant keeps the existing epoch accuracy and the anchor attempt owns no split.
- **Number-only assignment:** the event-unique number resolves first to one registration, then only inside the split's candidate set. A candidate qualifies when it is still running, contains the participant, the participant is not excluded and holds fewer than N. One qualifies silently; several return `SPLIT_CHOICE_REQUIRED` and require `chosenAttemptId`; none returns `SPLIT_CANDIDATE_UNAVAILABLE` with one reason per candidate. An attempt that started after the split's capture instant is never a candidate and never receives the split.
- **Event ledger:** a block-free capture and its assignment use `timing_event_capture_receipts`; an accepted assignment also writes an attempt receipt with `timing_capture_origin = SPLIT_ASSIGN`. Closure coverage reads `CAPTURE` receipts only, so assignment sequences never hide or create a capture gap. `timing_event_audit` keeps capture, undo, cancellation and refused assignment history.
- **Closure gate:** a queued split blocks every still-running candidate, not only the attempt displayed at capture; a non-candidate or later-starting attempt is never blocked. The refusal names the split, its capture instant and all blocked candidates.
- **Legacy queue:** pending `ANONYMOUS_SPLIT` marks are backfilled into the split/candidate tables with `legacy_mark_id` and one candidate; their mark is never rewritten, they stay assignable only in their captured attempt and block only that attempt.
- **Pass storage:** a split-derived pass is a new `TimingMark` in the resolved attempt with `source_split_id` unique, `clock_epoch_id` null, `device_sequence` null, raw and official columns equal to the capture instant and the elapsed value in that attempt's scale. Its assignment sequence lives on the `SPLIT_ASSIGN` attempt receipt, the event receipt and the audit, never in the mark's capture sequence slot. `source_split_id` is the database-level one-assignment guarantee; SQL checks keep either an epoch or a source split and forbid a capture sequence on a split-derived mark.
- **Schema:** `migrate_manual_timing_3_2026_11_14.sql` (+ down) adds `timing_event_splits`, `timing_anonymous_split_candidates`, `timing_event_capture_receipts`, `timing_event_audit`, `timing_split_state`, `timing_capture_origin`, the receipt-key `origin` and the nullable/split-provenance mark columns. The down file refuses to run while split-derived passes exist.
- **UI:** the «Текущий блок» selector is removed; the number field captures with no block, the queue shows every candidate's label and elapsed value, and an ambiguous number opens an explicit choice dialog with no pre-selection. Every user-facing label of the timing page calls a block-free capture «отсечка» (the queue, its action buttons, the capture field hints and the API refusals); routes, types and stored tables keep the `split` naming. The attempt record drawer is titled «Запись категории» for a category and «Запись заезда» for a heat; inside it the record is called «запись», and its closure actions are «Завершить хронометраж» and «Отменить хронометраж».

## MANUAL-TIMING-4 delta

- **Offline scope:** offline timing is capture-only. A device without a server answer records capture-side entries (a number or tile press, an anonymous split, a split assignment, a pending-finish bind and a withdrawal) in its device outbox and keeps them across a page reload while storage is durable. Closure, forced closure, cancellation, capture lock transfer, publication, protocol and result changes are online-only and are not offered while the page has no connection.
- **Derived, not stored:** the server read model stays confirmed-only. The page derives unsent rows, per-participant and per-block pending marks and the page totals from the outbox plus `TimingSurfaceView`; nothing pending is written as a pass, split, result or place.
- **Tile and block cues:** a participant with pending marks keeps its state fill and label and shows `confirmed+pending/N` (for example `1+1/2`), a dashed violet outline and the badge «не отправлено: N»; its accessible name states both counts. A block with pending marks shows «Отметок не отправлено: N» and replaces the ready-to-finish prompt with a pending notice. The unsent accent is violet, never the exclusion/cancelled red or a participant state colour.
- **Page figures:** the top controls show «Не отправлено: N» counting every unsent entry of every kind, «из них отметок: K» equal to the sum of tile marks, and the connection state. «Отправить сейчас» is offered while any unsent entry exists; a refused entry is reported once and discarded, so the queue and the figure contain unsent entries only.
- **Send queue:** the block is shown only while at least one unsent entry exists **and is not currently being sent**; an ordinary online press whose request is still in flight does not flash it, and an empty queue is absent. When it appears, every settled unsent entry is listed with its kind, block, participant number and name where the kind has them, its press clock and its state. A server refusal is reported once in the field/status area and its outbox row is removed; no rejected row persists. Entries send automatically in press order on reconnect and on page open, and the `Idempotency-Key` header keeps retries at most once.
- **Number field:** a press that passes local validation clears the field and keeps focus immediately, whether the server confirms or does not answer; a later refusal shows «Отклонено: № N — reason» and never restores the number. Local validation failures keep the typed value. An unsent press is never reported as a failed or lost capture. The busy, refusal and status feedback share one fixed-height slot, so a press never changes the card height or shifts the blocks below.
- **Withdrawal pair:** quick undo of the newest unsent capture drops its pending mark at once and keeps the capture and withdrawal adjacent in the queue; the pair contributes no tile or block marker. A withdrawal of a confirmed capture is reported separately and never lowers the confirmed count; if its capture is refused, the withdrawal is not sent.
- **Reload and volatility:** durable storage restores every unsent entry of every kind and resends it after reload. When the device falls back to memory, the page warns «Записи хранятся только в этой вкладке. Перезагрузка или закрытие страницы их потеряет.» while unsent entries exist and asks the browser for leave confirmation; after a loss the page shows only confirmed server values.
- **No server-contract change:** no new route, response field or database column is introduced by this delta.

## Breakage signals

- A second `RUNNING` attempt of one category or one heat, or an attempt identified by a label or position.
- A capture accepted from a workstation without the event capture lock, or a silent claim on Start.
- A pass without a receipt/sequence, a duplicate sequence creating a second pass, or completion with a receipt gap.
- A raw capture changed by a correction, or an official-time correction without a reason/administrator.
- A completed closure that publishes a partial result set, a place, a registration-only result, or a synthetic time/split for a DNS/DNF participant.
- A published participant with fewer or more than N splits, a cumulative N different from the total, or a split changing a place/tie/order.
- A heat attempt writing an ordinary `Result`, `result_splits`, a place or a CMS advancement, or missing a per-lap heat time for a multi-lap start; a heat closure changing stage/qualification; a category attempt writing a heat time; a heat closure missing an at-N heat time, overwriting an existing heat time or publishing partially.
- Timing exposing a race-section row in a **category** attempt, or blocking ordinary result entry for a simple sibling section.
- A closure blocked by another attempt's mutation, a definitively refused capture-side action, a capture-less attempt or an all-excluded attempt.
- A start-number change allowed during a running attempt, or a race created over a running-attempt participant.
- The 100 ms display making a network request or changing an official value; a collapse of one protocol block changing a sibling.
- `ORGANIZER` without `REFEREE` offered a timing surface, or `REFEREE` unable to time any event.
